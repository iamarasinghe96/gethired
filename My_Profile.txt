STORY 1: The ESG Evidence Library — Automation & Problem Solving
Best used for: Project management, data/analytics, automation, admin, operations, environmental roles

Rather than applying pressure, I handled it through technology and creative thinking.
I built macros from scratch — after hundreds of test runs, they never failed once after deployment. I developed Excel templates for day-to-day analysis, QC templates, troubleshooting macros for data input issues, and a monitoring and search dashboard for the entire library. Individual access to sensitive files was coordinated via email with parent account managers. After analysis and PAM approval, evidence emails were saved back into the library automatically.
The entire workflow — which would normally require a full web/cloud-based solution team — was built by me, solo. The project was completed within four months with only ten analysts, including myself, with three team members sick for two weeks. No pressure was put on any individual.
